## What completeness means here

The listed Gmail searches have no remaining pages. This is a searched collection, not an account export or a guarantee that every recruiting-related message in every channel was found. Messages that use none of the search terms and involve an unidentified sender may be absent. LinkedIn exposes partial thread history as it loads; the capture includes the outgoing messages read in those threads, not a certified export of all LinkedIn history. SMS, WhatsApp and other accounts were not searched. Attachments and recruiters' incoming text are not included.
